How It Works

03 / Trust & Reliability

Trust & Guardrails Achievement

Verified & Secure

We designed StackPulse with built-in engineering guardrails so that every briefing is factual, secure, and respectful of your time:

🛡️
Automated CVE Security Audits

Daily cross-referencing with active National Vulnerability Database (NVD) CVEs. If an open flaw hits a dependency in your stack (e.g. PyTorch, FastAPI), you get immediate notice before deploying.

🔍
Primary Source Verification

Announcements are sourced directly from authenticated lab blogs (OpenAI, Anthropic) and original Git repositories—preventing synthetic hallucinations or third-party rumor mills.

🧠
7-Day Zero-Duplicate Memory

Memory tracking logs all previously delivered content IDs over a rolling 7-day window. You are guaranteed never to see the same repository or article repeated.

🔒
Strict Privacy & Zero Tracking

No advertising trackers, no sponsored click-pixels, and zero data sharing. Your email and preferences are solely used to deliver your personal briefing.

💬 Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about StackPulse and your daily briefing.

What sources does StackPulse monitor?

We continuously ingest updates from GitHub Trending, official AI research releases (OpenAI, Anthropic), active CVE security databases, and high-signal developer discussions on Hacker News.

How does personalization work?

You simply select the languages, frameworks, and topics you care about. StackPulse matches the day's top updates against your profile so you only read stories that matter to your stack.

When do I receive my email?

Your briefing is delivered at 8:00 AM every morning (or weekdays if you prefer). It takes just 2 minutes to read over morning coffee before your standup.

Can I change my preferences or unsubscribe?

Yes! You can enter your email on this page at any time to update your topics, or click the 1-click unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email.
